Hello good people! I am Alina. I am currently working as a software engineer at **OpenRefactory, Inc**. I graduated from the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology** [(BUET)](https://www.buet.ac.bd/web/#/) in March 2025. My undergraduate thesis was supervised by [Dr. Md. Monirul Islam](https://cse.buet.ac.bd/faculty/faculty_detail/mmislam), on convolutional feature fusion for content-based image retrieval, published at [IEEE ICMI 2026](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/conhome/11539750/proceeding). 
Currently, I am looking for PhD opportunities.

My research interests lie in the **security and privacy of machine learning, networked systems, and software supply chains**, particularly what happens when a system depends on code, data, or participants it cannot fully verify. Professionally, I build tools for VEX report generation and CVE reachability analysis through dependency chains and call graphs. I am currently researching attacks on federated learning, with a focus on model poisoning in 5G handover prediction. I also have hands-on experience in convolutional feature aggregation through my work on content-based image retrieval, published at IEEE ICMI 2026.
