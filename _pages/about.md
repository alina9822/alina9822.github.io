Hello good people! I am Alina. I am currently working as a software engineer at **OpenRefactory, Inc**. I graduated from the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology** [(BUET)](https://www.buet.ac.bd/web/#/) in March 2025. My undergraduate thesis was supervised by [Dr. Md. Monirul Islam](https://cse.buet.ac.bd/faculty/faculty_detail/mmislam), on convolutional feature fusion for content-based image retrieval, published at [IEEE ICMI 2026](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/conhome/11539750/proceeding). 
Currently, I am looking for PhD opportunities.

My research interests lie in **deep learning** and the **security of distributed machine learning and software supply chains**. I have hands-on experience in convolutional feature representation and evaluation through my work on content-based image retrieval. My security interest centers on what happens when proprietary systems depend on external code or data. Professionally I work on tools for CVE reachability analysis through dependency chains and VEX report generation, and I am currently exploring poisoning attacks and non-IID data heterogeneity in federated systems which are typically deployed in high-stakes settings where data cannot be pooled.
