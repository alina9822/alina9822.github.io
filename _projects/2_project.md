**Technology & Tools:** React.js, TailwindCSS, Spring Boot, STOMP, Ollama (llama3.2)

I built this website to support individuals during difficult times, inspired by platforms like The Help Line. It aims
to connect people with therapists in Bengali and offers a safe space to share thoughts and struggles. The platform
also includes a chatbot powered by LLaMA 3.2 for instant support.
