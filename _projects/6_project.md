**Technology & Tools:** Graphics, OpenGL

Three graphics assignments that progress from basic rendering to a full ray-traced renderer.

## Key Work

- **OpenGL Rendering & Animation** — built 3D scenes with primitives, transformations, camera setup, and animated motion, including rolling/board-like scenes and cube/cylindrical object constructions.
- **3D Transformation Pipeline** — read scene data from text files and applied matrix transforms (translation, rotation, scaling, projection); processed triangle data and generated outputs for multiple pipeline stages, including z-buffer checks.
- **Ray Tracing & Shading** — built a renderer that shoots rays from the camera through pixels, intersects them with spheres and triangles, applies ambient/diffuse/specular lighting, and exports the final images as BMP files.

> Progressed from basic OpenGL drawing to a 3D transformation pipeline and finally to full ray-traced rendering with lighting — the core stages of computer graphics.
